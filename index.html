What this is

AltKlin takes tools people usually pay for and rebuilds them from scratch as free, open-source software. Clean-room: the behaviour is redesigned from what the tool should do, never from the original code.

Every AltKlin tool has zero dependencies, runs on your own machine, and needs no account.

Vektor

Gran Turismo 7 · €0 · Windows & macOS

Turn any PNG into Gran Turismo 7 livery decals — SVG files under the 15 KB limit, without losing the drawing.

Problem
GT7 only accepts SVG decals up to 15,360 bytes. Generic vectorizers go over the limit or wreck the details.
Output
Flat-color shapes only, in one file when possible — otherwise a few files stacked in the same frame for perfect alignment.
Quality
Tries several curve precisions, keeps the closest match to your image, drops the least visible details first.
Colors
Change, merge, swap or remove colors; remove the background in one click.
Privacy
Everything runs locally. No image ever leaves your computer.

Principles

  1. Free, for real

    No trial, no premium tier, no watermark. MIT license.

  2. Clean-room

    Rebuilt from what the tool should do, never from someone else's code.

  3. Zero dependencies

    Nothing to install around it, nothing that breaks when a package disappears.

  4. Yours, offline

    Runs on your machine, without an account or an internet connection.
